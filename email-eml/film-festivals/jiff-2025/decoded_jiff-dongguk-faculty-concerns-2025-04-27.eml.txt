존경하는 전주국제영화제 조직위원회께,

전주국제영화제의 끊임없는 한국 영화 발전에 대한 기여와 예술적 무결성에 대한 헌신에 깊은 감사를 표합니다. 젠더워치독 리서치 콜렉티브는 귀 영화제가 올해 프로그램에 관한 중요한 정보를 공유하기 위해 연락드립니다.

저희는 귀 영화제에 참가하는 동국대학교 영화영상학과 교수진들과 관련하여 심각한 우려를 전달하고자 합니다. 이는 영화제의 평판과 무결성을 보호하기 위해 조직위원회가 즉시 인지해야 할 사안입니다.

동국대학교의 광범위한 성폭력 문제와 국제적 결과

동국대학교 영화영상학과는 현재 다음과 같은 매우 심각한 문제에 직면해 있습니다:

• 성폭력 사례에 대한 제도적 대응 실패 기록
• 여성 전임 교수 전무
• 한국의 미투 운동이 한창이던 2018년에 여학생회 폐지
• 성폭력 및 성폭행에 대한 독립적 신고 시스템 부재

이러한, 구조적 문제로 인해 국제적 파트너십에도 심각한 영향이 초래되고 있습니다:

• 캐나다 대학 1곳이 이미 동국대학교와의 교류 협약 갱신을 거부
• 미국 대학 2곳이 현재 동국대학교와의 관계를 재평가 중
• 글로벌 대학 랭킹 기관이 이 문제를 경영진에게 보고한 상태

이러한 상황은 다음과 같은 방식으로 귀 영화제에 영향을 미칠 수 있습니다:

1. **평판 위험**: 국제 언론이 이 문제를 보도할 경우, 영화제가 성폭력 문제가 있는 기관의 대표자들에게 플랫폼을 제공했다는 비판에 직면할 가능성
2. **국제 참가자 우려**: 국제 게스트나 심사위원들이 관련 교수진과 함께하는 행사에 참여하는 것을 꺼릴 수 있음

Dear Jeonju International Film Festival Organizing Committee,

We deeply appreciate JIFF's continuous contribution to Korean cinema and commitment to artistic integrity. Gender Watchdog Research Collective is reaching out to share important information concerning your festival program this year.

We wish to express serious concerns regarding the participation of faculty members from Dongguk University's film program in your festival. This is a matter that requires immediate attention from the organizing committee to protect the festival's reputation and integrity.

DOCUMENTED CONCERNS THAT COULD IMPACT JIFF'S REPUTATION

Our extensive research has documented systemic sexual violence within Dongguk University's graduate film program. We bring this to your attention not to disrupt your festival, but rather to help you avoid potential controversy that could overshadow JIFF's artistic achievements. Specifically:

INSTITUTIONAL PROBLEMS AT DONGGUK UNIVERSITY:

• No visible, independent reporting system for sexual violence and sexual assault
• Zero full-time female faculty in the graduate film program
• Abolition of the Women's Student Council during Korea's MeToo movement (2018)
• Documented pattern of institutional negligence in responding to sexual assault cases:
  - Took no action for 6 months after being notified about a professor's sexual assault of a student
  - Only implemented a token "professor replacement policy" 9 months after an assault occurred
  - Failed to take disciplinary action against a professor until formal prosecution occurred

INTERNATIONAL CONSEQUENCES ALREADY UNFOLDING:

• One Canadian university has already declined to renew its exchange agreement with Dongguk University
• Two U.S. universities are currently re-evaluating their partnerships with Dongguk
• A global university rankings organization has escalated concerns to management level

POTENTIAL IMPLICATIONS FOR JIFF:

This situation creates several potential risks for JIFF:

1. **Reputation risk**: International media coverage could criticize the festival for providing a platform to representatives of an institution with documented sexual violence issues
2. **International participant concerns**: International guests or jurors might be reluctant to participate in events alongside implicated faculty
3. **Conflict with progressive values**: JIFF is known as a progressive, socially conscious festival, and this association could undermine that image

SUGGESTED ACTIONS TO PROTECT JIFF'S REPUTATION:

We respectfully suggest the following actions to protect JIFF's reputation:

1. **Review participation**: Carefully review the participation of Dongguk University faculty in panels and events
2. **Balanced representation**: If Dongguk faculty participation continues, ensure panels include voices advocating for reform in film education
3. **Due diligence statement**: Consider preparing a statement about the festival's commitment to due diligence regarding participant selection
4. **Safety commitment**: Use this as an opportunity to reinforce JIFF's commitment to safety in film education and production

The global film industry is experiencing a profound reckoning with issues of power, exploitation, and sexual violence. As a prestigious international festival, JIFF has the opportunity to demonstrate leadership by ensuring those given platforms at the festival represent positive change.

We would welcome the opportunity to discuss this matter further and can provide additional documentation if needed. Our aim is to help JIFF maintain its excellent reputation while avoiding potential controversy.

For your reference, our comprehensive documentation is available at: https://genderwatchdog1.github.io/timeline-website/index.html

Respectfully,

Gender Watchdog Research Collective
